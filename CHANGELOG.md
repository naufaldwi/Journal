# Changelog & GitHub Release Notes

All notable updates for **Journal** are documented here. You can copy any section below directly into your **GitHub Release Notes**.

## v1.1.5 — Social Life, World Events & Relationship Depth Update

### What's New
- **Romance Overhaul & Gendered Indonesian Names**: Added a one-time sexual orientation choice before seeking romance, three distinct dating paths (*Dating App*, *Blind Date*, and *Friend-to-Partner*) with realistic rejection odds affecting mood and closeness, a strict anti-cheating check preventing multiple active partners, annual closeness drift when relationships are neglected, and gender-accurate Indonesian naming pools.
- **Multi-Child Families**: Removed the single-child limit so couples and families can welcome or adopt multiple children across generations.
- **Office & Professional Dynamics**: Added a Career screen breakdown displaying colleague, manager, and teacher bonds alongside a visual **Life Miserable** warning indicator showing how hostile ties affect happiness and stress.
- **Dynamic Annual Global News Feed**: Added yearly world dispatches covering wars, scientific breakthroughs, and cultural shifts tailored dynamically to the active year and country/region.
- **10-Year Mood Trends Visualization**: Added a visual mood-trend chart on the Health screen plotting Happiness, Stress, Anxiety, career stress load, and random encounter markers over the past 10 years.
- **Social Stream Feed**: Characters (Age 13+) can post **Life**, **Career**, and **Relationship** updates and receive popularity-scaled likes plus supportive or critical comments from NPCs.
- **Daily Random Events**: Added trait-tailored daily encounter popups (stumbling upon cash, finding lost items, running into old acquaintances) with choices that shift mood, stats, and bonds.
- **Relationship Settings Dashboard**: Added a dedicated tie-priority manager (accessible from **Activities** and **Relationships & Bonds**) to assign NPCs to *Standard*, *Interact*, or *Gift ($150)* priorities, filter by group, run quick check-ins/gifts, and batch-boost prioritized bonds.

## v1.1.0 — Calm Submenu UI & Usability Update

### What's New
- **Simplified 4-Tab Navigation**: Streamlined the main bar down to **Journal**, **Activities**, **Tree**, and **Profile** so every feature stays easy to reach without clutter.
- **Calm Story-First Home Screen**: Focused the **Journal** screen on your character summary, active crossroads choices, and chronological story feed—moving **Health & Routine** and **Daily Missions (Age 13+)** into clean one-tap bottom sheets.
- **Activities Submenu Hub**: Grouped **Education & Careers**, **Skills & Adult Loans**, **Creator Studio**, **Underworld**, and **Relationships & Bonds** into spacious submenu cards with glanceable live status and native back-button support.
- **Focused Career & Creator Sub-Views**: Split **Education & Careers** into *Current & Schools* and *Career Tracks*, and organized **Creator Studio** into *Live*, *Channel*, and *Monetize* tabs for smoother scrolling.
- **Documentation & Disclosure Updates**: Added a prominent **Google Sign-In & Firebase** disclaimer in `README.md` alongside updated `v1.1.0` sideload instructions.

## v1.0.0 — Initial Public Release

### Core Gameplay & World
- **14 Playable Countries with Cultural Representation**: Localized cities, naming pools, current regional events, and culturally accurate syndicates across **Indonesia** (*Jakarta, Yogyakarta, Bandung, Surabaya, Denpasar, Medan, Makassar*), Japan, Hong Kong, Italy, United States, United Kingdom, South Korea, Portugal, Denmark, India, Brazil, Australia, Canada, and Nigeria.
- **Skill Progression & School Auto-Boosts**: 8 character skill bars (*Deduction & Law*, *Engineering & Logic*, *Painting & Visual Art*, *Music, Guitar & Vocals*, *Acting & Stagecraft*, *Athletics & Racecraft*, *Culinary & Craft*, *Persuasion & Rhetoric*), 8 dedicated training activities, and automatic school skill growth (including **Law School**, **Art & Design Conservatory**, **Music Conservatory**, **University**, and **Craft & Motorsport Institute**).
- **Careers, Fame & Underworld**: Classic & fame career tracks (*Law & Advocacy*, *Fine Artist*, *Actor*, *Model*, *Solo Singer*, *Band Guitarist / Drummer*, *Race Driver*, *Software*, *Medicine*, *Culinary*, *Editorial*) with skill gates and natural fame decay, plus country-specific crime syndicates (*Preman*, *Triad*, *Yakuza*, *Mafia*, *Gangster*, etc.).
- **Creator Studio & Live Streaming**: Niche selection, trend riding, follower replies, sponsorships, merch drops, studio gear upgrades (`✦`), and interactive live broadcasts.
- **Health, Finances & Life Progression**: Physical and mental wellness meters, diet/exercise/sleep routines, random & historical era events, non-negative cash (`>= $0`), adult-only loans (Age 18+, 4% annual interest), year-by-year aging milestones, multi-generation **Family Tree**, and **Daily Missions** unlocking at **Age 13+**.
- **Accounts & Persistence**: Sign in with **Google** for **Firebase** cloud saves and public leaderboards, or play offline anytime in local **Guest Mode**.
